Your access to this website has been restricted due to automated activity.

Why this happens. Collier County property records are public and we want them to stay easy for everyone to use. Two things force us to limit automated collection: it slows the site down for every other visitor, and the personal information in these records is increasingly harvested in bulk — often with automated and AI tools — to impersonate people and commit identity fraud.

This includes AI assistants. Asking a tool such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot to look something up on this site reaches us as automated access. It is not permitted under our Terms of Use (linked at the bottom of this page).

If you reached this page during ordinary use of the site, we apologize for the inconvenience. To request that access be restored, start by verifying your email address below.
